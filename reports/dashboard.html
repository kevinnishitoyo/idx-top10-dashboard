Method

Activity and foreign-participation signals are shown separately. Pullbacks require a five-session rising MA20 regime and use stops below the latest five-session swing low. Rows that fail the setup rules retain reference levels but hide R:R and are labelled Watch only. Trade scenarios use a days-to-weeks horizon.
